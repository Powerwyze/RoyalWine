01 / CONNECT

Add the server-side credentials

Open the Royal Wine project’s environment settings. Add these for Production and, if needed, Preview. Keep the values in Vercel; never paste them into this page, the task sheet, email, or source code.

Open Vercel settings ↗
  • OPENAI_API_KEYAn OpenAI project key with access to the configured voice and image models.
  • WYZER_GMAIL_USERThe authorized Gmail sender address.
  • WYZER_APP_PASSWORDThe sender’s Google app password.
  • ENABLE_FACE_HOST=trueAlready set on this project. Keep it enabled.

Optional image settings: OPENAI_IMAGE_MODEL (default gpt-image-2), OPENAI_IMAGE_SIZE (default 768x1152), and OPENAI_IMAGE_QUALITY (default low). An already configured Resend sender is also supported by the app.

02 / REDEPLOY

Apply the new environment

In Vercel Deployments, redeploy the current production deployment after saving the credentials. Return here and choose “Check again.” These checks show configuration presence; they do not validate account access, quota, or delivery.

03 / TEST LIVE

Meet Blue before your guests do

  1. Refresh the kiosk, then enable camera sentry once and allow camera and microphone access.
  2. Check the spoken greeting, live captions, guest count, and explicit readiness before the five-second countdown.
  3. Generate one portrait. Check the people and result, and listen for Blue’s question, relevant Bartenura or Black Irish fact, and follow-up while it develops.
  4. Spell a test email address you control. Correct it on screen and tap “Confirm & email photo.” Confirm receipt in that inbox.
  5. End the visit and verify the photo and email clear. Verify sentry can welcome another visitor after inactivity.

Live voice, image generation, and photo email have not been verified without credentials. Treat this as a completed build awaiting activation, not an event-ready service.
